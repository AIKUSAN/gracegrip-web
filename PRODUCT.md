# GraceGrip product record

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

The installable PWA is the source experience for an Android Trusted Web Activity. A separate iOS app is a later decision.

## Users

Adults worldwide who want private help with a behavior or substance use they have chosen to change. They may arrive during an urgent moment, without an account or onboarding. GraceGrip is openly Christian and usable without choosing prayer or Scripture.

## Product Purpose

GraceGrip offers immediate, nonclinical self-help, guided reflection, practical next steps, and optional spiritual encouragement. The seven launch focus areas are alcohol; pornography and sexual habits a person wants to change; anger and conflict; nicotine and vaping; gambling; gaming and digital habits; and other drug use. Stress, loneliness, grief, and relationship pressure are related support topics. Choosing a focus area does not label someone as having an addiction.

## Positioning

GraceGrip joins private, immediate help with optional Christian practice and a user-chosen path. Useful tools and reviewed articles remain available without an account, payment, AI, or a live community session.

## Operating Context

People may use GraceGrip on a phone in an exposed or stressful moment. Help Now must open directly before onboarding. A brief safety choice must route overdose, withdrawal, violence, threats, and abuse away from an urge timer or puzzle to fixed guidance and professional help. Country selection can help find relevant resources; without it, direct people to local emergency services and a country-searchable helpline directory.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Keep the existing six mobile destinations visibly labeled and add a floating page-navigation control for new destinations. Help Now remains one tap away.
- Keep the current encrypted device-local journal and readable JSON backup flow. The historical streak remains legacy progress until a person explicitly creates a goal. Do not upload prior progress by default.
- Offer optional goals, private progress, daily plan, activity charts, permanent botanical emblems, and an optional tile puzzle. The gambling path offers the score-free calm mode; public rankings are deferred.
- Public Resources articles are build-time content reviewed by a human. Editorial drafts are private, and only owner approval may create a publishing pull request.
- Optional membership uses passkeys, recovery codes, and optional verified email. Cloud sync covers only selected goals, check-ins, and emblems after explicit opt-in; the journal stays on the device.
- A live text-only community operates only during owner-hosted sessions. Posts require server-side checks and owner approval before broadcast; AI may flag but never approve. No direct messages, media, or outbound links at launch.
- The reflection helper is a limited pilot. It has no browsing, tools, automatic journal access, publication authority, or emergency authority. Reviewed help tools work without it.
- All core support stays free. Giving is voluntary through the existing external links.
- The current Cloudflare release remains public until the 2.0 web and Android release gates pass. No partial public rollout.

## Brand Commitments

The GraceGrip name, existing logo artwork, and protected tagline are fixed trademark assets. Do not alter their files or wordmark. The voice should be calm, direct, hopeful, and free of shame or claims of guaranteed recovery. GraceGrip's Christian identity is clear; prayer and Scripture are choices.

## Evidence on Hand

The current Next.js static-export app, six routes, feedback Function, local journal and backup code, and a Clear Current gallery concept are available in the repository and sibling design worktree. The Stitch folder is layout inspiration, not product copy or identity. The WHO ASSIST self-help guide informs goal and reflection structure for adults without severe substance problems or dependence. Qualified substance-use and safeguarding reviewers have not yet approved the new danger guidance or guided content.

## Product Principles

1. Make immediate help available before setup.
2. Let people define their own goals without diagnosis or public comparison.
3. Give accurate limits and practical routes to human help.
4. Keep sensitive activity private by default and make sharing explicit.
5. Use faith as an invitation rather than a requirement.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support keyboard and screen-reader use, enlarged text, reduced motion, mobile safe areas, and English-language adults across countries. Do not assume a country, gender, diagnosis, or belief.

## Open operational decisions

Owner-hosted community hours, the verified transactional-email provider, qualified clinical/safeguarding reviewers, production AI capacity, and the Play developer account are release prerequisites. Their absence must not be represented as a live capability.
