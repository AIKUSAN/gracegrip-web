# GraceGrip 2.0 visual system

## Direction

**A · Clear Current** is the approved base. The interface reads like a calm, useful field guide: the next helpful action appears first, a compact set of tools sits beside it, and personal progress waits below. Stitch contributes the practical dashboard structure, not its identity or claims.

Use the existing self-hosted Manrope body and Libre Baskerville accent until a reviewed visual comparison approves a replacement. The existing logo files and wordmark remain untouched. Spacing runs on a 4px base; surfaces use a restrained 11–16px radius. Use borders for separation and one soft shadow on the raised phone navigation dock.

## Navigation and hierarchy

- Keep the existing six routes in a labeled floating phone dock. At ordinary phone width use two rows of three; at narrow width or enlarged text reflow to three rows of two. The dock sits above the bottom safe area and page content clears it.
- New destinations open from a separate floating **Pages** control, not by hiding an existing tab. The six established destinations remain one tap away.
- On desktop, lead with Help Now, focus-area choice, and a visible tool list. Use a compact side panel for today's optional actions. Personal charts and emblems stay below those actions.
- The safety choice on Help Now is before any timer, puzzle, or suggestion. Danger routes are plain, high-contrast, and free of animation.

## Surface modes

- Home: persuade a first-time visitor to take one useful step, then operate for returning people.
- Help Now, goals, plan, community, helper, editor: operate.
- Focus guides and Resources articles: read.

## Interaction rules

Controls name the action they take. Optional prayer and Scripture are explicitly labeled. Do not turn a missed day, a setback, or an urge into a failure score. Keep the published interface honest about limits: no live community when the owner is away, no AI when the device or quota cannot support it, and no country-specific emergency claim without verified local data.
